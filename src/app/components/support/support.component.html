Support from ABC for Health

ABC for Health is a Wisconsin-based, nonprofit public interest law firm dedicated to linking children and families, particularly those with special needs, to health care benefits and services.

ABC for Health can assist or help refer Wisconsin residents who

  • Are having trouble paying medical bills
  • Have lost or will lose health coverage
  • Need help determining which health coverage is right for them
  • Need help determining eligibility for health benefits
  • Have a pre-existing condition and can’t find health care coverage
  • Are interested in applying for coverage programs such as BadgerCare Plus
  • Received a claims denial or are in a dispute with their private insurer
  • Received a denial of coverage from BadgerCare
  • Have questions about having BadgerCare and private insurance at the same time
  • Have questions about using their benefits
  • Would like to know how health care reform will affect their coverage
